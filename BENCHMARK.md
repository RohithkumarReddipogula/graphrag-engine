## 1. Setup

- Test split: 375 questions from the 2WikiMultihopQA validation split (300 multi-hop, 75 per type, and 75 single-hop), answered against a pooled corpus of gold and distractor paragraphs.
- Run once, at git tag `m5-frozen` (commit 01ea234), settings hash d0fac230b47d; run-once lock `results/m5/TEST_RUN.lock`: finished (2026-10-09T06:09:40+00:00 to 2026-10-09T06:29:46+00:00).
- All choices (retrieval configuration, entity resolution threshold, graph share) were made on the dev split; the test split was not used before this run.
- Generator: `openai/gpt-oss-120b` on OpenRouter endpoint `deepinfra/bf16`, temperature 0.0, reasoning effort medium, 1500-token context. Every system may answer "unknown" (scores 0).
- Hybrid baseline: BM25 + `intfloat/e5-base-v2`, weighted fusion alpha 0.9 (title off), `BAAI/bge-reranker-base` reranker.
- Graph: entity and relation extraction, entity resolution, Neo4j graph; graph retrieval with at most 5 seeds, 2 hops, hubs above degree 25 never passed through, top 10 paths; graph share g = 0.5 in `graph_plus_chunks_g0.5`.
- Scoring: official 2Wiki v1.1 EM/F1 with Wikidata aliases; 95% percentile bootstrap CIs (10,000 resamples, seed 0); paired bootstrap for differences.

## 9. Limitations and cost

- Pooled corpus: questions are answered against one corpus of gold and distractor paragraphs, unlike the standard 2Wiki distractor setting, so absolute numbers are not comparable with published leaderboards; the answer scoring is the official one.
- The same model (gpt-oss-120b) extracts the knowledge graph and answers the questions; its extraction and answering errors may be correlated. The judge is a different model family.
- The language models have likely seen Wikipedia; the closed-book baseline and the closed-book-wrong subset account for that, and the gains hold on that subset (section 2).
- Single-hop questions are generated from templates, which favours lexical retrieval; the regenerated single-hop set was not hand-checked a second time.
- Some 2Wiki gold triples link a mention to the wrong same-name page (found during entity resolution), and some single-hop questions are scored against the gold answer only, without aliases.
- Entity resolution keeps precision first: some duplicates remain (for example United States in two clusters), see `results/m3/resolution_report.md`.
- "unknown" scores 0. On two-way comparison questions a guess would often be right, so abstaining systems can score below guessing ones; the unknown rate is reported for every system.
- Per-type results rest on 75 test questions each; secondary comparisons are exploratory.
- EM follows the official 2Wiki normaliser, which keeps Unicode dashes; a few correct answers written with a non-breaking hyphen score EM 0 (section 8). The judge counts them correct.
- Judge: agreement with the hand check 29 of 30; the hand-check first pass was drafted by Claude, a different model from the judge and the generator, and reviewed by hand.
- Cost: the test run 0.112 USD, the judge 0.017 USD; all OpenRouter calls of the project up to the end of M5 1.82 USD (6,148 calls; `results/m5/spend_snapshot.json`, taken from `results/spend/openrouter_calls.jsonl`).
